SureShot Website

Project Website + CMS
Role Web Developer
Team Size 3 people
Duration 10 weeks

Context

During the second year of my Bachelor's program, two of my classmates and I were tasked with finding a company to create a website for. Through a relative, we got into contact with SureShot and got the opportunity to create the new SureShot website. The project required us to create a website based on the wishes of SureShot and we delivered on that. However, we did not want to just make a website. We wanted a future-proof website that anyone at SureShot would be able to adjust at any given moment. For this reason, we also created a CMS (content management system) from scratch. Lastly, we took the responsibility of launching the website to make sure everything was running as it should.

Frontend

The website is made up of several pages: home, about, the brands, the tools, the kitchen, the team and jobs & contact. The home page gives the reader a small introduction and contains a mosaic component that displays different brands that SureShot works with. The about page explains the history of SureShot. The brand page displays a gallery that automatically shifts and links towards partners of SureShot. The tools page gives insight into the tools used by SureShot. The kitchen page explains SureShot's product strategy. The team page displays a picture of every employee within SureShot and their title. Finally, the jobs & contact page allows readers to come into contact with SureShot and enables them to potentially land a job at SureShot.

Backend

SureShot does not have a team of developers, which means that we found it necessary to create a CMS, to make sure no developers are needed to maintain the website. This not only allows any employee within SureShot to edit the website, but also makes sure the website is not at risk of becoming outdated quickly. Obviously, not everyone should be allowed to access the CMS, which is why it is not visible to readers and an account is needed to access it. Accounts are also protected with two-factor authentication to protect the CMS even further. Once an employee has logged in, they can select any page on the website and start editing it. Every page is built using components, which makes it very easy to add and remove pieces of content. Furthermore, every existing component may be edited, as well as the metadata of the page. Lastly, to increase SEO (search engine optimization), the employees are forced to fill in information about, for example, images, which is then used to fill in the alt text, meta title and meta description of the image.

My Responsibilities

During the development of this website, I was mainly responsible for the accounts, the brands page, the individual brand page and the team page. I used Microsoft Identity to create a custom environment where I can create accounts, protect them with two-factor authentication and add important settings like resetting the password or changing the connected email address. For the brands page, I created the gallery component which allows readers to click on any partner of SureShot and read about them. To make sure that no unnecessary work was being done, the individual brand page is simply a template page that gets built based on the chosen partner. Lastly, I created the team page and integrated it into the CMS (the team page is the only page that was added later in the process and was not a base requirement from SureShot).
