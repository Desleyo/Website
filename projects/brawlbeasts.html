About the Game

Brawl Beasts is an online multiplayer party game in which you can play with up to 5 players. Each round will be played on a randomly chosen map and with a randomly chosen game mode. You can choose your favorite animal-like character, and customize them however you like. You can choose different skins and equip various items to make your character stand out from the rest.

Gameplay

Players can create their own lobby in the lobby creation menu and change settings like: maximum number of players, maximum number of rounds and whether it is a public or private lobby. Inside the lobby, the players can choose and customize their favorite character. When the majority of the players are ready, the game will start. Before every round, the loading screen will display information about the map, the game mode, the controls and general tips. The default round duration is two minutes, and after every round, the player with the most points will be displayed on screen. After every round is played, the victory screen will display the results and crown the player with the most points as the winner.

Game Modes

The game features the following game modes: Tail grabber, Brawl, King of the hill and Wack-a-mole. In Tail grabber, players obtain points for each second that they have the golden tail. Other players can steal the golden tail by bumping into the current holder. In Brawl, players obtain points for every player that they manage to get off the map. To assist the players and add to the fun, items will randomly spawn throughout the map. In King of the hill, players obtain points for every second they stand inside the yellow circle (hill), but the hill changes location every so often. Every player is given a bat which they can use to knock other players away from the hill. In Wack-a-mole, players obtain points for every mole they manage to hit with their hammer. Moles will randomly pop up from the ground for a brief moment, allowing players to hit them.

My Responsibilities

During the project, I was responsible for most of the multiplayer aspects of the game. Photon Pun was used to accomplish this. I used Photon Pun to synchronize player positions, round information and to create a lobby where players can see each other and ready up. I was also responsible for the game manager. The game manager randomly picks a map and game mode, manages the round timer and updates the scoreboard. Furthermore, I created the wack-a-mole game mode and was responsible for creating a dynamic camera that zooms in and out, based on player positions. Lastly, I was responsible for the UI functionality and created the functionality for the character customization.
