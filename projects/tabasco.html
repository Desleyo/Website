SureShot Tabasco Website

Project Website
Platform Shopify
Role Junior Developer
Duration 6 weeks

Context

After finishing the SureShot website project, we were asked by SureShot if we would like to work for SureShot as Junior Developers, during the summer break of 2025. I accepted this opportunity and started working for SureShot for the duration of the summer break. SureShot had come to an agreement with Tabasco, one of their partners, about making a Shopify website based on the new Tabasco website's design. Me being the only developer at SureShot meant that I was responsible for this project, and most of my time at SureShot was devoted to this project.

The Process

The SureShot Tabasco website contains a couple of pages that are heavily inspired by, or even copies of, pages on the actual Tabasco website. This might sound very easy, just copy and paste the HTML and CSS, but that is not what was expected of me. The SureShot Tabasco website is made using Shopify, which meant that I had to translate the pages I needed into Shopify's programming language called Liquid. Furthermore, I had to maintain a line of communication that allowed me to request specific images and videos that I needed for the SureShot Tabasco website. Lastly, I also had to add pages that actually allowed customers to buy the products (i.e. product page and checkout page).
